# Sparkbuild Construction – Next.js Web Platform & Admin Ready

A 1:1 identical reproduction of [https://www.sparkbuild.in](https://www.sparkbuild.in) built with modern **Next.js 15 (App Router)**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**.

---

## 🏗️ What Was Built

### 1. Complete Page Ecosystem (12 Routes)
- **Home (`/`)**:
  - Top Bar with direct dial, email, and social media icons.
  - Sticky Navigation with mobile drawer menu and "Get In Touch" action button.
  - Interactive 5-slide Hero Carousel with background images and CTAs.
  - Trust Badges (ISO Certified, 9+ Years of Expertise, 95%+ Satisfaction Rate).
  - Infinite Marquee Ticker highlighting construction verticals.
  - About Section with real certifications, team collage, and company statistics.
  - Full-Spectrum Services Grid (Architectural, Commercial, Healthcare/Hospital, Industrial).
  - "Built to Last" Dream CTA banner with consultation booking trigger.
  - 4-Step Engineering Work Process (Survey, Design, Construct, Handover).
  - Featured Projects Showcase with interactive category tabs (`Mega`, `Domestic`, `Facade`, `Interior`).
  - Client Video Testimonials with click-to-play YouTube modal (using real video IDs).
  - Proof of Excellence Awards (2024, 2023, 2022, 2021) and ISO 9001:2015 certification.
  - Client Review Slider & Testimonials.
  - "Why Sparkbuild" Proven & Trusted features (10-year structural warranty, local knowledge).
  - Interactive FAQ Accordion.
  - Newsletter Subscription.
  - Complete Footer with Jabalpur headquarters address, contact info, and legal links.
  - Floating WhatsApp chat button and sticky "FREE Expert Consultation" bar.
  - Interactive Consultation Booking Modal with automated lead recording.
  - Fullscreen Image Lightbox Modal for HD project inspections.

- **About Us (`/about`)**:
  - Company journey since 2016 in Madhya Pradesh & Pan-India.
  - Vision, Mission & Values.
  - "Real Foundation" & What Drives Us.
  - High-impact statistics (1000+ Projects, 95% Satisfaction, 110% Commitment).

- **Services (`/services`)**:
  - Detailed breakdown of Residential, Commercial, Healthcare & Hospital Facilities, and Renovation/Remodeling.
  - 3-Step Process (Plan & Discover, Design & Quote, Build & Deliver).
  - Service-specific inquiry triggers.

- **Projects / Portfolio (`/projects`)**:
  - Filterable portfolio by Category (`Mega`, `Domestic`, `Facade`, `Interior`) and Status (`Completed`, `Upcoming`).
  - Project specifications, budgets, energy ratings, and key highlights.
  - Instant image zoom modal.

- **Our Team (`/our-team`)**:
  - Founder & Director spotlight: Er. Pankaj Sen.
  - Leadership bios: Chief Structural Consultant, Operational Manager, Quality Execution Lead, and BIM/Drafting Head.

- **Wall of Love (`/wall-of-love`)**:
  - 260+ Google reviews (4.95 of 5 rating).
  - Verified client video walkthroughs.
  - Homeowner & business reviews.

- **Blog (`/blog`)**:
  - Architectural, civil remodeling, and structural checklists.
  - Dedicated interactive reading modal for complete articles.

- **Careers (`/careers`)**:
  - Why Work With Us & Founder's Speak.
  - Active job openings: *Site Supervisor*, *Project Manager*, and *Junior Architect*.
  - Job Application modal with resume/portfolio URL and profile capture.

- **Contact (`/contact`)**:
  - Inquiry form with validation and quick inquiry capture.
  - Direct office contact details and embedded Google Map for Vijay Nagar, Jabalpur.

- **Schedule a Meeting (`/appointment-letter`)**:
  - Dedicated Book Your FREE Expert Consultation step-by-step form.

- **Legal (`/privacy-policy` & `/terms-condition`)**:
  - Complete compliance documents.

- **Admin Portal (`/admin`)**:
  - Dedicated admin dashboard to preview, track, and manage:
    - Consultation leads
    - Contact messages
    - Job applications
    - Project portfolio
    - Lead data export as JSON

## 🚀 Running the Project

### Development Server
```bash
npm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

### Production Build
```bash
npm run build
npm run start
```
